Who We Are

Built on Faith, Fuelled by Love

Tusobola Kids Foundation was born in the heart of Masese — one of Uganda's most vulnerable communities in Jinja. Founded by Swaliki, a movement artist and mentor, we show up every single day for children who need someone to believe in them.

We pay school fees, provide meals, supply clothing and train children in performance arts — because we believe every child deserves more than survival. They deserve a future.

Faith
Everything we do is rooted in God and His love for children.
Education
Every child deserves the opportunity to learn and grow.
Community
We create a family where every child belongs.
Transparency
Every contribution is stewarded with honesty and integrity.
"No child should be defined by poverty or circumstance — but by the gifts and purpose God has placed within them."
— Swaliki, Founder of Tusobola Kids Foundation
Our Vision
To see every vulnerable child in Uganda grow into a confident, educated, healthy and purpose-driven individual.
What We Do

Our Programs

We meet the whole child — body, mind, spirit and talent — through six core programs running daily in Masese, Jinja.

📚
Formal Education

We pay full school fees, development fees and examination fees for all 28 children. We also provide school uniforms, shoes, bags, books and all required supplies.

School Fees Uniforms Books Exam Fees
🍽️
Feeding Program

We provide nutritious daily meals and refreshments during all our programs. No child should have to learn or train on an empty stomach.

Daily Meals Nutrition Refreshments
🎭
Performance Arts

Children are trained in Breaking (B-boy/B-girl), Afro Dance, Contemporary Dance, Tricking, Gymnastics and Acrobatics by experienced coaches and peer mentors.

Breaking Tricking Gymnastics Dance
🧠
Mentorship & Development

We run regular circle sessions focused on communication, leadership, team building, mental well-being, problem solving and life skills.

Leadership Mental Health Life Skills
✝️
Faith & Character

Children are encouraged to grow spiritually through prayer, Bible teaching, worship and Christian mentorship while respecting every child's background.

Prayer Bible Study Character
👕
Clothing Support

We provide clothing and essential personal items to children who are most in need — because dignity matters as much as education.

Clothing Essentials Dignity
